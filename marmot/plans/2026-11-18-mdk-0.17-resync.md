- **Fair scheduling narrowed to admin and same-account intents** (#450). Local scheduling only.

The three race vectors now replay. The runner uses MDK's simulator account keys
(`deterministic_nostr_keys` over `pad32(account)`), so `tip_committer` compares the keys the vectors
were recorded with, and checks `convergence_decision` through the new
`ConvergenceResolution.decisiveRule` (`BranchSelector.decisiveRule`). Replaying them found a real
convergence bug: the incumbent branch was dropped whenever this client had authored its tip,
because the graph replayed our own commit from its parent, which MLS cannot do (the UpdatePath is
not encrypted to its committer), and because canonical commits were paired with retained states by
position, which the unrecorded founding Add shifts. So in a race we were half of, we always yielded
to the peer's branch. The graph now takes a canonical commit's result from the retained window
(`knownResults`) and pairs commits with states by epoch. Both activation races pass. The
own-device-removal race reaches the expected states, but alice-phone, whose leaf the losing commit
removes, reports no decision: a commit that removes us replays to a state whose GroupContext has not
advanced, so that branch never forms. The test asserts exactly that gap. Closing it needs mls-kmp
to advance the public GroupContext past our own removal.
